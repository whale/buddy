# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People who need a calm focus companion, explicitly including ADHD users (README.md).
Buddy stays in a Mac menu-bar drawer; its iPhone companion keeps the same task list nearby.

## Product Purpose
Help people choose a small amount of work, remember what they are doing now, and
recognize completed work without losing tasks during interruptions or device sync.

## Brand Personality
Calm, gentle, playful. Existing copy and README describe a quiet companion, with
optional parrot celebrations and “Donezo” completion feedback.

## Anti-references
Avoid interruptive focus stealing, unexplained missing tasks, and controls that
become unreadable on the red escalation background (CLAUDE.md and STATUS.md).
Do not turn this settings change into a redesign of Buddy's established interface.

## Design Principles
- Preserve tasks and unfinished edits before changing presentation.
- Mirror shared task behavior across Mac and iPhone; keep Mac window controls local.
- Use the established three-level escalation tokens on every card control.
- Prefer quiet defaults and explicit user action over automatic interruption.
- Verify actual native behavior rather than assuming browser tests prove it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Existing project requirements: keyboard operation, accessible hit targets, readable
normal/warning/red states, reduced-motion support, and legible text with scrolling
rather than clipping when content cannot fit. No new compliance claim is implied.

Source: README.md, CLAUDE.md, STATUS.md, and the confirmed settings implementation request.
